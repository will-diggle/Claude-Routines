About

William Diggle

William Diggle is a British tenor based in Passau, Germany, where he is a member of the ensemble at Landestheater Niederbayern. William trained at the Royal College of Music (Master’s, 2017–2019) and the Royal Academy of Music (Bachelor’s, 2013–2017).

In the 2025/26 season, William will perform roles in Eine Nacht in Venedig (Strauss), Trial by Jury (Gilbert & Sullivan), Gianni Schicchi (Puccini), Turandot (Puccini), Parsifal (Wagner), and more.
